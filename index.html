Long videos in, short clips out.

Splicebay turns long videos from partner creators into short, captioned vertical clips, then uploads the ones we approve to our TikTok accounts as drafts.

  1. Cut. A long video a creator has given us permission to use is transcribed, and its strongest moments are picked and cut into 9:16 clips with captions and a credit to the creator.
  2. Review. Every clip is watched and approved or rejected by a person, and its caption is checked before anything leaves Splicebay.
  3. Upload. Approved clips are sent to the matching TikTok account as drafts. They're finished and posted from the TikTok app.

Splicebay is a private tool used to manage TikTok accounts owned by its operator. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok.

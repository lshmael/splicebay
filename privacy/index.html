Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Splicebay ("Splicebay", "we", "us") uses, why, and the choices you have. Splicebay is a private tool that runs on its operator's own computer. It creates short clips from long-form videos and uploads them, as drafts, to TikTok accounts owned by its operator. It is not offered as a public service, and it is not affiliated with TikTok.

Information we get from TikTok

When a TikTok account is connected to Splicebay, the account holder signs in on TikTok's own page and chooses whether to grant access. Splicebay asks for only two permissions:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): the account's TikTok ID, display name and profile picture. We use these only to show which account is connected and to send each clip to the right account.
  • Upload to drafts (video.upload): lets Splicebay send a video and caption to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is published until the account holder finishes and posts it in the TikTok app.

Splicebay does not read followers, messages, comments, likes, watch history, analytics or any other content from TikTok accounts.

Access tokens

After an account is connected, TikTok gives Splicebay access and refresh tokens. They are stored only on the operator's computer and are used only to upload clips the operator has approved. We never see or store TikTok passwords.

Videos and transcripts

Splicebay works with long-form videos from creators who have given us permission to use their content. These videos and their transcripts are processed on the operator's computer. To decide which moments make the best clips, the transcript text of the source video may be sent to an AI service provider (Anthropic or OpenAI) under that provider's terms. No TikTok account information is ever sent to these providers.

How we share information

We do not sell information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. Information leaves the operator's computer only:

  • to TikTok, when an approved clip and its caption are uploaded to a connected account; and
  • to an AI service provider, as transcript text of source videos, as described above.

How long we keep it

Tokens and profile details are kept until the account is disconnected. Clips and transcripts are kept on the operator's computer until they are deleted.

Your choices

  • Disconnect in Splicebay: disconnecting an account deletes its tokens and profile details from Splicebay.
  • Revoke access in TikTok: you can remove Splicebay's access at any time from the app permissions section of your TikTok account settings.
  • Ask us: contact us to ask what we hold or to have it deleted. We'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Splicebay runs on a private computer and is reachable only over a private, encrypted connection. No method of storage or transfer is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold.

Children

Splicebay is not directed to children and is not used to collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
